Anu’s Recipe Book

188 handwritten pages · recipes · transcribed & searchable

The week ahead

Tap “add a dish” on any day, or let the book choose. Your plan feeds the shopping list automatically.

Cook with what you have

List what’s in your fridge and pantry — the book ranks every recipe by how much of it you can already make. Basics like salt, oil and everyday spices are assumed.

Shopping list

Built from your week planner — one group per planned dish. Tick things off as you shop.

Write a new page

Add your own recipe to the book — a family favourite, something you’ve adapted, or a dish you want to remember. It joins the search, the planner and the fridge matcher like any other recipe.

Keep a copy

Recipes and photos you add live in this browser only. Save a backup to keep them safe, move them to your phone, or send them to family — then load it back here any time.
